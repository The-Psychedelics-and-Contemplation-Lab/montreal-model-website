## Montreal Model Workshop!

<figure>
  <img src="/images/workshop-2025-auditorium.jpg" alt="A lecture hall full of workshop participants facing a projected slide about the Montreal Model." width="1600" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</figure>

On May 29th and 30th, 2025, we hosted the inaugural two-day workshop on the Montreal Model of ketamine therapy for treatment-resistant depression. Bringing together 80 healthcare professionals from across disciplines and around the world—including physicians, psychologists, nurses, and researchers—the event was designed to share the origins, philosophy, and practical structure of the model. Grounded in nearly a decade of public healthcare experience, the workshop offered a deep dive into each phase of our integrative protocol, from evaluation and preparation to dosing sessions, music selection, and integration. Participants were introduced not only to the model’s scientific underpinnings and clinical rationale, but also to the lessons we’ve learned in applying this work with real-world, complex patient populations.

A heartfelt thank you to our incredible team of volunteers whose dedication made the workshop possible, and to all the attendees who joined us and brought it to life.
